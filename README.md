This is a simplistic chess tutor app with difficulty settings.  Easy, Medium , and Hard. Each setting is different , both easy and medium contain highlighting to show each movement a piece can perform. It also showcases which pieces can eat or be eaten if you select a piece . Easy has a fairly easy Ai with it often not preserving high level pieces and often feeding. Its made like this to allow new players to better understand moves and concepts of the game . Medium pieces are preserved much more and the Ai is smarter . Harder the AI uses stockfish but its a more difficult AI with the highlights being turned off . At the moment all promotions are automated to Queen.
